# Challenge Intake

1. Gather official rules, clarifications, the challenge statement, supplied assets, and deadline.
2. Separate explicit requirements from assumptions and design ideas.
3. Identify starting state, engineering objective, dominant artifact, evaluation contract, and realization/proof mode using [Challenge Classification](../core/CHALLENGE_CLASSIFICATION.md).
4. Record what must be real, what is simulated, mocked, unavailable, editable, or protected.
5. Identify mandatory technologies, standards, safety constraints, interfaces, deliverables, and the shortest credible proof of the central claim.
6. Record the normalized approved interpretation, scope, proof needs, and realization boundary in `problem.md` using the [Proof Model](../core/PROOF_MODEL.md).
7. Identify ambiguities, event constraints, acceptance/scoring expectations, and claims not yet supported.
8. Stop for human approval before solution design.

Do not introduce domain assumptions before authoritative source material exists.
